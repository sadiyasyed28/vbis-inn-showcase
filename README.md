# VBIS Inn — A Premium 3-Star Showcase

A high-converting, premium digital showcase designed exclusively for **VBIS INN**, the premier 3-star hotel located in Melekote, Tumakuru, Karnataka.

This digital brochure acts as a virtual showcase of the hotel's warm hospitality, modern comforts, and local accessibility, built to pitch a refined lodging experience and drive direct bookings.

## 🏨 Hotel Pitch & Vision

VBIS INN is designed to elevate the standard of hospitality in Tumakuru. By blending regional comfort with modern aesthetics, the hotel offers a seamless stay for business travelers, spiritual pilgrims, and holidaying families.

*   **Refined Accommodations**: Tastefully configured AC and Non-AC spaces designed for quiet relaxation.
*   **Authentic Culinary Experience**: Daily complimentary South Indian breakfast (featuring fresh idlis, crispy dosas, and signature filter coffee) along with an in-house restaurant and rooftop lounge.
*   **Convenient Location**: Nestled within Melekote, providing direct access to the highway, local landmarks (Mandaragiri Hills, Siddaganga Mutt), and business hubs.
*   **Exceptional Service Standards**: 24/7 front desk support, dedicated basement parking, and a clean, safe environment.

---

## 🎨 Design System & Visual Philosophy

The showcase is designed to feel like a high-end editorial travel magazine, employing clean typography and spacious layouts.

*   **Typography**:
    *   *Headings*: `Instrument Serif` — evoking a classic, luxury-brand editorial aesthetic.
    *   *Body*: `Work Sans` — clean, modern, and highly legible.
*   **Color Palette** (OKLCH Color Space):
    *   `Brass` (`oklch(0.72 0.115 78)`): Hints of warm metal tones represent hospitality and premium service.
    *   `Ink` / `Foreground` (`oklch(0.24 0.028 155)`): Deep, slate-teal elements deliver reading comfort and visual depth.
    *   `Background` (`oklch(0.982 0.011 88)`): Warm, natural off-white texture creates a welcoming atmosphere.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Key Features

*   **Framework**: [React](https://react.dev/) + [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/) + [TanStack Router](https://tanstack.com/router)
*   **Styling**: [Tailwind CSS v4](https://tailwindcss.com/) (using CSS-first `@theme` configuration)
*   **Icons**: [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
*   **SEO & Metadata**: Optimized meta descriptions, Open Graph protocol, canonical tags, and structured JSON-LD data for Google Rich Results.
